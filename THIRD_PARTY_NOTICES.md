## Not distributed

The ir3 shader variants used by `tools/a16gpu_render.py` and
`tools/a16gpu_p6p7.py` (`tools/a16gpu-render/render-shaders.json`,
`tools/a16gpu-p7/p7-shaders.json`) are compiled from shaders shipped with
macOS, so they are not part of this repository. Generate them on your own Mac
with `tools/a16gpu-render/make-render-shaders.sh` and
`tools/a16gpu-p7/make-p7-shaders.sh`. No Apple, Qualcomm, ASUS or Microsoft
firmware or binaries are included.
